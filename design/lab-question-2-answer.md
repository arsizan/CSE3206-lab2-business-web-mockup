# Lab Question 2 — Script-ready answer

**Course:** CSE 3206 — Web Engineering Sessional
**Project:** Modern Business Website Mockup — Northline

## Project overview

The client needs a clean, modern and trustworthy business website design before development begins. The design will help visitors understand the business, explore its services and start a conversation. Northline, a fictional professional-services consultancy, is used as the demonstration brand because the brief does not supply a business name, sector, logo or brand assets.

## Scope and deliverables

1. Home page with a clear hero, services overview and prominent call-to-action buttons.
2. About page explaining the business, values and approach.
3. Services page with three service areas, benefits and frequently asked questions.
4. Contact page with clearly labelled fields, service selection and a confirmation-state demonstration.
5. Desktop layouts at 1440 px and mobile layouts at 390 px.
6. Eight high-fidelity layouts and eight low-fidelity wireframes.
7. A consistent colour palette, typography system, reusable buttons, service cards, navigation and form-field components.
8. Organised, editable Figma source created with the provided import kit, plus SVG layouts and source photography.
9. Up to two reasonable rounds of revisions within the agreed scope.

## Budget and assumptions

The fixed design budget is **£192**, matching the posted project budget. It includes the mockups, responsive layouts, reusable components, exports and two reasonable revision rounds. Public Google Fonts and Unsplash photography avoid paid font or stock-image costs. A free Figma account is sufficient for a single design file, subject to Figma’s current plan limits.

Website development, domain registration, hosting, paid plugins, commercial stock, copywriting, email delivery and ongoing maintenance are not included. Extra pages or a major change in direction would require a separate estimate.

## Design and usability decisions

The forest-green, ivory and terracotta palette communicates trust and warmth. Instrument Serif gives headings a distinctive editorial character; Plus Jakarta Sans keeps navigation, body text and forms readable. A consistent layout, clear labels, visible focus states and repeated conversation CTAs guide visitors towards making contact. Mobile layouts stack content and provide a compact navigation menu.

## Handoff

The downloadable kit includes a local Figma importer, 16 SVG layouts, assets and documentation. Running the importer in Figma desktop creates editable frames, live text, vectors, image fills, shared styles and reusable components. The student can then use **File → Save local copy** to obtain a native `.fig` file. The ZIP itself is not a native `.fig` file.

This is a lab design prototype, not a deployed consultancy business. Partner names and contact details are illustrative; the form demonstrates interaction without sending or storing data.
